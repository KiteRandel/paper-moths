# Amara & the Paper Moths

**An evening rooftop run, a game by [KiteRandel](https://github.com/KiteRandel).**

Amara chases glowing paper moths across a sepia town at dusk. Leap the gaps between roofs, duck under the laundry lines, dodge chimneys and crates, and catch moths in a row to build up your combo.

**Play it:** https://kiterandel.github.io/paper-moths/

## Controls

| | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change lanes | ← → or A D | swipe left/right |
| Leap | ↑, W or Space | swipe up or tap |
| Slide | ↓ or S (in the air: drop fast) | swipe down |

Press **Esc** (or the ⏸ button) to pause and unpause. Hold **↓ / S** to keep sliding for as long as you like.

## Things to know

- Your first run starts with a quick tutorial: time slows right before a row of crates and a laundry line, and waits until you press jump or slide.

- Every moth you catch flutters into your **moth purse** in the bottom-right corner. Golden moths count as 5. The purse is saved between runs.
- 🩹 A **bandaid** absorbs one bump. You can only have one at a time.
- 🎀 A **ribbon** whisks nearby moths straight into your purse for a few seconds.
- 🛍️ Every so often a pink **SHOP** sign stands over one lane. Run through its curtain to visit a little shop where a very bored, very big pink Amara sits on a tall stool and sells 🩹 bandaids for 300 moths and ✂️ paper scissors for 500. The scissors snip away the next obstacle coming at you in your lane. You can have 1 bandaid and up to 3 scissors. Click (or tap) an item on her counter to buy it, and hit the big Leave shop button (or Esc) to head back to the roof. Skip the shop and she takes it personally: she floats along over the rooftops for about 20 seconds, sitting in mid-air with her cheek in her hand, teasing you and lazily flicking grey-white lightning down onto the roof ahead. A crackling ring marks where each bolt will land, and the scorched spot it leaves still hurts for a moment, so switch lanes. There's always a lane that's safe.
- Things get faster the longer you run. The top-left corner counts the bosses you've defeated this run, with a badge for each boss that stays greyed out until you beat it, and your record for most bosses is saved in your browser.

## Bosses

Every few hundred metres a boss swoops in over the rooftops. The first time you meet each boss in a run, Amara keeps running on empty rooftops while a guide shows how to beat it, with pictures of each attack. Press Space twice (or tap the button twice) when you're ready to fight. You get a free bandaid at the start of each fight.

- **The Twin Lanterns**: a blue Amara and an orange Amara, flying ahead of you in each other's arms. They send waves rolling back down the roofs at you.
  - 🔵 **Blue wave:** keep running and don't touch anything. Jumping, sliding or changing lanes as it passes hurts you.
  - 🟠 **Orange wave:** slide under it.
  - After each round of waves they drop a golden paper lantern into one lane. Catch it and Amara flings it back at them. Three hits and they drift apart.
- **Wrath & Joy**: furious red Wrath (steaming, trembling, growling, and angrier with every hit) smashes the roof while giddy yellow Joy dances around her, giggling, flitting between twirls, cheers, claps and dances.
  - Cracks flash across the roof before each slam. 🔴 **Red crack:** Wrath smashes a crater there, and stepping in it hurts, so switch lanes or jump it. 🔥 **Flaming crack:** that crater erupts into a pillar of fire too tall to jump, so you have to switch lanes. The angrier she gets, the more fire. 🟡 **Gold crack:** Joy cannonballs down and plants a golden sunflower spring.
  - **Bounce on Joy's spring** to launch high into the air straight at Wrath. She tries to dodge into another lane, so steer in mid-air and smack into her. Watch out: when a gold crack **flickers red**, Wrath is about to stomp that spring flat into a hole, and she does it more often the angrier she gets. The angrier she gets, the more red cracks per row. After your second hit Wrath snaps and grabs Joy by the throat: for 10 seconds every spring is crushed and the roof shatters around you row after row, so find the safe lane each time. Then Joy kicks free and plants one last spring for the finishing blow. Three hits and Wrath runs out of fury, and Joy hugs her as they float away.
- **Lilac & Glitch**: composed purple Lilac sings while twitchy green Glitch strains at the leash in her hand.
  - Each note of Lilac's song points to a lane: 🎵 low = left, middle = centre, high = right. A glowing note floats over each lane as she sings.
  - Then Glitch replays the song as strikes, tearing down those lanes in the same order and rhythm. Be out of the lane on the beat!
  - **Sing it back:** rows of note orbs appear across the roof. Run through the right lane for each note to echo the melody. A perfect echo cracks the leash, and one wrong note means listening again. Three perfect echoes snap the leash for good. Songs get longer and faster each time.

Beating a boss earns a big score bonus and a shower of moths.

- **Ivory & Onyx**: prim, polite Ivory and fast, sloppy Onyx play chess, and the rooftop becomes their board. On each sister's move her squares glow and hurt, so stand on the other colour. Then she drops a piece that attacks the way it moves: rooks slide down a lane, bishops cut diagonally, knights hop in L-shapes and pawns march in a wall. Then it's your move: race your pawn down eight ranks of glowing squares to the gold line (one hit and you're back to rank 1) to promote to a queen. The sisters drop into the lanes and dodge, so get in line with one and press jump to strike. Three captures is checkmate.
- **Neon Amara** (final boss): every Amara you've faced floats in at once. They spin in a wheel ("With our powers combined!") and fuse into one glowing white Amara with a neon rainbow outline. She throws a ball at you: a white ring shrinks around your feet, so jump the moment it turns gold to knock the ball back. She returns it faster every time, and between returns the Amaras inside her team up in new pairs: a burning wave (the twins and Wrath), a burning song (Glitch and Wrath), a rippling chessboard whose spikes flip as a ripple runs across it (the twins and Ivory & Onyx) and a knight's waltz (Lilac's song and the knights). Each one calls out her part as she attacks, and when you land a hit they bicker about whose fault it was. She throws the ball in the middle of her attacks, so you're dodging and timing your jump at once, and sometimes one jump has to clear a crater or a knight and hit the ball back together. Break the rally and the combo starts over. In the last 7-hit rally you're chained to the middle lane, and with every throw a ghost of one of the bosses sends her own attack down your lane: hold still through a blue wave, slide an orange one, or jump a crater or knight right as the ball arrives. Win a 3-hit rally, then a 5-hit rally, then the 7-hit one, and her colours scatter.

### The ending

Beat Neon Amara and Amara slows to a stop on the roof. Each pair comes down to say goodbye in their own way: the twins hug, Wrath grudgingly claps while Joy cheers, Lilac sings one last little song, and Ivory & Onyx bow. Pink drops in too, with something to say about how much you spent at her shop. Then they all turn into streams of coloured paper moths that spiral into Amara, each one lighting a badge and adding a stripe to her new rainbow outline, and she flies off to the moon while the credits drift past as moths. The end card shows your run. **Run again** starts from scratch, and **Loop** sends you round the same game again, keeping your moths and badges. (Click the moon on the card to see where everyone ended up.)

## Art direction

Everything is drawn to match Amara's illustration. The palette is sampled straight from her texture, objects use soft toon shading, and an ink-outline pass gives every shape the same brown lineart. A paper-grain sepia finish goes over the whole frame. The textures (roof tiles, brick, plaster walls with lit windows, crates, leopard-spot laundry and paper lanterns) are painted in code. While you run, a slowed, softened, reverb-washed version of "Rush Hour" plays; boss fights fade in "Dreamtone Bloom". Sound effects are synthesized live.

Amara (`assets/amara.glb`) is KiteRandel's character, modeled, rigged and animated in Blender, with run, leap, slide and stumble animations made for this game, plus flying, embracing, slamming, singing and glitching animations for the bosses.
